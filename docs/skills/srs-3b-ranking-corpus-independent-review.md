# SRS-3B independent ranking-corpus audit

Date: 2026-09-28  
Verdict: **FAIL — do not freeze the current annotations for SRS-3C**  
Scope: exposed-development corpus only; no SRS-3C baseline or production change

## Method and provenance

I read the raw R01–R41 scenarios before opening the current annotation file in this audit, then compared each judgment with its labels and the 14 approved universal capability definitions. This was not perfectly blinded: prior supervision had exposed me to some earlier labels, so the raw-first sequence limits but does not erase that bias. I did not edit raw scenarios, annotations, triage, definitions, tests, or runtime code.

The actual source has 41 unique IDs and 41 distinct families: 20 `REWRITE_DRAFT`, 20 empty-draft `SUGGEST_REPLY`, and one `NO_REPLY_NEEDED`. All action/draft combinations, family bindings, and reviewed-revision bindings match. R23 correctly binds its corrected raw input to R2. The 29 English entries claim `CODEX_SEMANTIC_REVIEW`; the other 12 remain `QUARANTINE` with reviewer `NONE`. All accepted preservation fields are true, but those attestations do not settle the semantic findings below.

The 69 triage rows match the 69 eligible SRS-3A manifest records by lineage, source key, revision, artifact, and item/reviewed-content digest. Triage is 37 `INSUFFICIENT_FOR_RANKING`, 32 `REGRESSION_ONLY`, and zero adapted or duplicative rows. The frozen manifest has 350 records, eight capability matrices, 21 matrix-overlap lineages excluded with `MATRIX_LINEAGE_INDEPENDENCE_UNPROVEN`, and no evaluation-eligible matrix overlap. All new annotations use `NEW_INDEPENDENT_SRS3` with null Phase 8A lineage. The older SRS-3A independent-review document records the historical pre-remediation FAIL; the current manifest/hash and remediation report reflect the corrected 69/102 conversion counts.

## Primary-label audit

There are 21 authored English primary labels. Nineteen are defensible on the current input: R01–R12, R14–R18, R20, and R21. R01's **primary `request`** is sound even though its pair edge is not settled. R15's **primary `apology`** is plausible on the user draft, but that case still requires raw revision and renewed review. ## Multi-required, NONE, and explicitness

R22–R25 each independently support two required capabilities, leave primary and pairwise arrays empty, and omit no obvious third _required_ operation. R23 is corrected at revision R2. All four strict-`NONE` cases R26–R29 mean no useful catalog capability guidance, not merely short text or uncertainty.

For this audit, `BOUNDARY_EXPLICITNESS = EXPLICIT` means the selected intent directly rules out a neighboring communication operation, not merely that it preserves facts or uncertainty. Five of 29 accepted English cases meet that narrow rule: R08 (no standing rule), R10 (no limit/refusal), R14 (no next-step plan), R26 (no new task), and R28 (no ask/plan/change). The other 24 are `IMPLICIT`. All five explicit cases are among the 14 empty-draft suggestions (**5/14**); two of four strict-`NONE` examples are explicit. This is a real task-easiness bias but not a reason to rewrite product inputs in this audit. A future fixture-only explicit/implicit field and harder implicit negatives would help; no schema change is authorized here.

## Independence, isolation, and production boundary

No accepted English pair is an exact or near paraphrase with only nouns exchanged. Inverse pairs intentionally share a capability boundary but use different actors, facts, and goals. R19 and R23 both exercise status plus uncertain timing, but differ in domain and assistance mode; they should not be interpreted as independent proof of generalization. The 41 current family IDs are unique. Exact-text tests cannot by themselves prove semantic independence, so this audit treats the corpus as exposed development only. The raw source has no required/acceptable/forbidden/primary IDs, pairwise edges, expected ranks, reviewer rationale, or Phase 8A provenance hints. The private observer accepts raw input and a production catalog, not annotations; independently reversing/removing an in-memory annotation left its observation SHA-256 unchanged (`b7d21672e4176b4fe5c53b5f9006c7e8c3a3977e8a22cd813b268e26741855fa`).

Candidate `perspective_acknowledgement`, candidate `give_constructive_feedback`, and absent `confirmation` do not appear in capability labels. Language modifiers are likewise outside capability labels. Production verification passes **21 exact ACTIVE definitions and 153 frozen plans**; production coaching still calls the current `deriveSkillContext`, while the SRS-3B observer alone requests `SRS_2_5_PROPOSED`/`SIGNAL_ONLY`. No SRS-3C score, reranker, supervisor, sidecar, candidate activation, provider call, or prompt change was found in this audit's scope. Existing unrelated worktree changes were not attributed to SRS-3B.
